# Quickstart: Slice 5 — Reviews

1. **Submit** — open a product → «اكتب تقييمك» → 4 stars, 25+ characters, name, «٠١٠١٢٣٤٥٦٧٨» → «شكراً! تقييمك هيظهر بعد المراجعة» → nothing public yet → same phone again → «انت قيّمت المنتج ده قبل كده».
2. **Moderate** — `/admin/reviews` (pending tab shows the review with phone) → approve with a reply → product page shows it with the reply; the card shows the stars and «تقييم واحد».
3. **Verified** — place and deliver an order for the product with phone 01098765432 → review from that phone → approve → «اشترى من المعرض» shows.
4. **Unpublish/delete** — unpublish → gone from the page, average updates; delete → the phone can review again.
5. **Checks** — Arabic + English at 320 px; view-source of the product page has `aggregateRating` only after approval.
